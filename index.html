Hello, my name isBrooke Bowman

and I’ve lived many lives. I’ve been a commercial fisherman, a sex worker, a drug addict. I spent 2.5 years homeless. Now I think a lot about happiness, community, culture, and how to create conditions where people are most likely to thrive. Some of that work finds expression through Vibecamp, a yearly gathering my Twitter friends and I first started dreaming up back in 2021.

Writing

I am writing a memoir! I may or may not post more personal essays as I continue along this journey—either way, following my Substack is probably the best way to stay updated if you’d like to read the book when it comes out in a few years. Or ten. We’ll see how quickly I can level up as a writer.

Gatherings

I run events that encourage people to be human together. The comms are often a little chaotic, and prestige is de-emphasized. Kindness and prosociality are centered instead. I’m currently only working on Vibecamp, though I would love to experiment with longer event formats one day.

Vibecamp

Vibecamp is a four-day gathering for the curious, kind weirdos of the world, held every June in Maryland.

Un-de-emphasizing prestige

Alright, look: I know I just said that I try to de-emphasize prestige, but this is my personal site, not event comms. Honestly, I’m mostly hoping a literary agent sees this site while I’m working on my memoir draft and decides yes, that’s the one. Or something. I’m not really sure how that world works yet.

Anyway, check out the time I told the NYT that peptides were making my hair fall out, the hilarious cartoon Infographics made based on the article, and that one time Rolling Stone referred to Vibecamp as “the bowels of the internet.”

Oh, and Vibecamp has received two Mercatus grants: one from their Program on Pluralism and Civil Exchange in 2023, and one from Emergent Ventures in 2024.
